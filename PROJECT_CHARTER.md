# GridPulse — Project Charter

## Decision problem
Grid operators and market observers need to understand not only when electricity demand is high, but when several operating signals become unusual at the same time: demand, ramping, forecast miss, local generation, and interchange dependence.

## Core question
**When does electricity demand become unusually difficult to forecast or serve, what operating conditions coincide with those hours, and how can those conditions be surfaced early enough for review?**

## Scope
- EIA-930 hourly balancing-authority data.
- Demand, day-ahead forecast, net generation, total interchange.
- Generation by fuel type as a second analytical table.
- Historical descriptive analytics, forecast evaluation, demand forecasting, anomaly/stress screening, and dashboarding.

## Non-goals
- Predicting blackouts.
- Replacing NERC, ISO/RTO, or balancing-authority reliability assessments.
- Claiming the screening score is an official emergency or reliability metric.
- Optimizing dispatch or market bids.

## Portfolio capabilities
Time-series data engineering, operational analytics, forecasting, anomaly detection, explainability, interactive dashboarding, and decision-support communication.
